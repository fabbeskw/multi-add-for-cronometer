Multi-Add for Cronometer

  1. Open https://cronometer.com/#diary and reload the tab once (the extension learns your session from the app's start-up traffic).
  2. Press Alt+Shift+M, or click the Multi-add button bottom-right, or MULTI next to FOOD in the diary toolbar.
  3. Type one food per line (200g chicken breast, 2 large eggs @lunch), press Find foods, check the preview, then Add all.

Nothing leaves cronometer.com. Undo and a diagnostics dump live in the panel.

Optional: the panel's TDEE tab estimates your energy expenditure from the food and weight you log. It reads nothing until you press Enable there.
